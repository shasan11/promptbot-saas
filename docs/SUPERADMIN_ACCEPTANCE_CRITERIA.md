# Superadmin Acceptance Criteria

The release is acceptable when:

- Every visible navigation item resolves without a placeholder page.
- Tenant primary subdomains can be created and edited with uniqueness validation.
- Plans and subscriptions remain manageable under their existing permissions.
- Payments can be recorded, reconciled, inspected, and refunded.
- Invoice status follows net linked settlement after refunds.
- Tickets can be assigned, prioritized, replied to, noted internally, resolved, reopened, and closed.
- Reports filter by date, separate monetary totals by currency, and export CSV.
- Website pages remain section-editable and publishable.
- System health reports live checks and supports permission-controlled queue/cache actions.
- General, security, email, SMTP, payment, and branding settings persist and apply at runtime.
- Sensitive values are encrypted and masked.
- Read-only administrators cannot perform write actions.
